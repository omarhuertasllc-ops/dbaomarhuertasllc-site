Know every Entra app registration before its secret expires.

Inventories Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD) app registrations, their client secrets and certificates into ServiceNow, with expiry tracking, ownership and advance renewal tasks.

Why Azure Inventory

The problem it takes off your plate.

Teams find out an Entra client secret or certificate has expired only when an integration, SSO login or automation breaks. Discovery and certificate tools don't see app-registration credentials, so expiry dates live in spreadsheets and calendar reminders, and nobody knows who owns each app.

Full app-registration inventory

Every Entra app registration, client secret and certificate pulled through Microsoft Graph into ServiceNow tables you can report on.

Expiry you see coming

Advance renewal tasks and dashboards for credentials expiring in 7, 30 and 90 days, before anything breaks.

Ownership that sticks

Each registration is tied to an owner and a service, so renewals land on the right team.

Read-only by design

The integration reads from Graph and never writes back to Entra. Credentials use ServiceNow Connection & Credential aliases.

CMDB-aware

App registrations become configuration items, so a credential outage maps to the services it affects.

Scheduled collection

A scheduled job keeps the inventory current with a full audit trail of every run.

How it will work

Four steps from install to value.

  1. InstallInstall Azure Inventory from the ServiceNow Store.
  2. Connect GraphAdd a read-only Entra app registration through a Connection & Credential alias.
  3. CollectRun the first collection, or wait for the scheduled one.
  4. Act on expiryWork the renewal tasks from the dashboard before anything expires.
